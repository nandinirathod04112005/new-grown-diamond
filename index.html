From Seed To Sparkle

The journey of a grown diamond

Six stages stand between a single carbon seed and a certified stone. Every one of them happens under our roof.

01
01

Diamond Growth

A diamond seed enters the reactor. Carbon settles on it atom by atom for weeks, growing a crystal identical to one formed deep in the earth.

02
02

Rough Diamond

The chamber opens to a rough crystal — raw, unrepeatable, and holding all of its light within.

03
03

Cutting

Master planners map every facet, then cleave the rough along invisible lines with sub-millimetre precision.

04
04

Polishing

Each facet meets the spinning scaife until it sits at the perfect angle — fifty-seven chances to bend light exactly right.

05
05

Quality Inspection

Gemmologists grade colour, clarity, cut and carat under 10× magnification before an independent laboratory certifies the stone.

06
06

Finished Diamond & Jewellery

The certified stone is set by hand into its final piece — ready for a lifetime in the light.
