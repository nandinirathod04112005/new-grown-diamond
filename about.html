New Grown Diamond

Excellence, grown with integrity

Built on a legacy of integrity and honesty, New Grown Diamond is synonymous with excellence and innovation in diamond manufacturing — serving customers worldwide with quality lab-grown diamonds while maintaining strong business values.

Today we are a manufacturer, wholesaler and supplier of CVD and HPHT lab-grown diamonds, working with B2B clients, retailers, jewellery traders and diamond buyers across the globe — with a focus on consistency in quality, quantity, supply and competitive pricing.

Our Legacy

Four Decades in Diamonds

Our story begins with natural diamonds. Across around four decades in the diamond industry, the team built its expertise the patient way — stone by stone, client by client.

That experience is the foundation everything since is built on. When the business moved into lab-grown diamond manufacturing, it carried decades of diamond discipline with it — the same standards, the same values, a new way of making.

The Milestone

2012 — The Transition to Lab-Grown Diamonds

In 2012, keeping pace with consumer and industry trends, New Grown Diamond ventured into the manufacture of polished lab-grown diamonds — recognising both the potential of the category and its ethical benefits.

Our Mission

A strong alternative to mined diamonds

With over 40 years of experience in mined diamonds, our team transitioned to lab-grown diamonds in 2012 — recognising the potential of the industry and the ethical benefits lab-grown diamonds bring.

Our commitment is to provide high-quality lab-grown diamonds while keeping pace with technological developments and innovation. Through awareness and education about genuine lab-grown diamonds, we aim to offer a strong alternative to mined diamonds — and to become a leading wholesale supplier of lab-grown diamonds.
