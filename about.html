Our Story

Grown with purpose

New Grown Diamond began with a simple conviction: the world’s most beautiful stones should not have to be taken from it.

So we grow them — real diamonds, crystallised from carbon in our own reactors, then planned, cut and polished by hands that treat each one as heritage in the making. Keeping growth, cutting and setting under one roof means a single standard runs from seed to showcase, and a story we can tell truthfully about every stone.

◎ Our Mission

Honest brilliance, within reach

To make certified lab-grown diamonds — and the jewellery they deserve — accessible with total honesty: transparent origins, independent grading and craftsmanship built to outlast trends.

◉ Our Vision

Every diamond, traceable

A world where every diamond can be traced to its first day — where brilliance is grown responsibly, certified independently and worn with pride in how it came to be.

Quality & Trust

Trust, documented

We never ask you to take brilliance on faith. Every stone is graded in-house first, then sent to independent laboratories to be graded again.

  • ◆ One uncompromising in-house standard at every stage of the process.
  • ◆ Independent certification by laboratories such as IGI and GIA.
  • ◆ A report that travels with the stone for the life of the diamond.
